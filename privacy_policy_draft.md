## 2. Information We Collect

### Account information

If you create an account, we collect information needed to create and manage that account, including:

- full name
- email address
- Firebase user ID
- account role, such as teacher or admin

Guest users can read published resources without creating an account. An account is required for features such as saving resources, earning download tokens, and downloading files.

### App activity and resource usage

We may collect app activity connected to your account, including:

- saved resources
- resources opened or read
- downloaded resources
- download token balance
- download token usage
- rewarded ad progress used to earn download tokens
- account settings such as whether a guide has been dismissed

This helps the app provide saved libraries, download history, token balances, and admin support.

### Files and educational resources

Admins may upload PDF resources to Firebase Storage so that users can read them in the app. Ordinary users do not normally upload personal files as part of the standard app experience.

When users download a resource, the app saves the selected PDF to the device Downloads folder under `EngRes Hub`.

### Advertising data

EngRes Hub uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to show banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect or use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics, depending on your device settings and Google/AdMob configuration.

Rewarded ads are used to let users earn download tokens. The app records rewarded ad progress so it can add a token after the required number of ads.

For more information about how Google handles ad data, see Google's advertising and privacy resources.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- create and manage user accounts
- allow users to read published resources
- let signed-in users save resources
- track download tokens and downloads
- provide rewarded ad token progress
- support account deletion requests
- maintain app security and prevent misuse
- help admins manage resources and app activity
- improve the reliability and usefulness of the app

## 4. How Information Is Stored

EngRes Hub uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage.

Account records, saved resources, download token records, resource activity, and deletion requests may be stored in Firebase. Uploaded PDF resources are stored in Firebase Storage.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the app, including:

- Google Firebase, for authentication, database, and storage
- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads, for advertising and rewarded ads

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the app, to prevent abuse, or to respond to valid legal requests.

## 6. Data Security

We use Firebase and Google services that support encrypted network communication. We also use app and database rules to limit access to user and admin data.

No system is perfectly secure, but we aim to protect user information using reasonable technical and organizational measures.

## 7. Account Deletion

If you create an account, you can request account deletion from within the app:

Profile > Request Account Deletion

After receiving a deletion request, we will review and process it. Deletion may include removing or anonymizing your account information, saved resources, download history, token records, and related app data, except where we are required or allowed to keep limited records for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

You may also contact us at [your support email] to request deletion or ask questions about your data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

EngRes Hub is intended for teachers and education professionals, not as a child-directed app. If this changes, the privacy policy and app settings must be updated to comply with child privacy and advertising requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at [your support email] so we can review and take appropriate action.

## 9. Ads and Download Tokens

Users may watch rewarded ads to earn download tokens. Watching rewarded ads is optional, but download tokens may be required to download files to phone storage. Published resources can be read in the app without downloading.

Rewarded ad views are counted only for the purpose of determining whether a download token should be added to the user's account.
