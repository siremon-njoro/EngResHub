EngRes Hub Privacy Policy

English Resources for Teachers

Last updated: 2026-08-22

EngRes Hub is an English teaching resource app designed to help teachers access, read, save, and download educational resources.

This Privacy Policy explains how EngRes Hub collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

Developer/operator: Siremon Digital Solutions
Contact email: siremondigitalsolutions@gmail.com
Country/region: Kenya

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create an account, we collect information needed to create and manage that account, including your full name, email address, Firebase user ID, and account role such as teacher or admin.

Guest users can read published resources without creating an account. An account is required for features such as saving resources, earning download tokens, and downloading files.

App Activity and Resource Usage

We may collect app activity connected to your account, including saved resources, resources opened or read, downloaded resources, download token balance, download token usage, rewarded ad progress, and account settings.

Files and Educational Resources

Admins may upload PDF resources to Firebase Storage so users can read them in the app. Ordinary users do not normally upload personal files as part of the standard app experience.

When users download a resource, the app saves the selected PDF to the device Downloads folder under EngRes Hub.

Advertising Data

EngRes Hub uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob to show banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect or use information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics, depending on your device settings and Google/AdMob configuration.

Rewarded ads are used to let users earn download tokens. The app records rewarded ad progress so it can add a token after the required number of ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To allow users to read published resources.
  • To let signed-in users save resources.
  • To track download tokens and downloads.
  • To provide rewarded ad token progress.
  • To support account deletion requests.
  • To maintain app security and prevent misuse.
  • To help admins manage resources and app activity.
  • To improve the reliability and usefulness of the app.

3. How Information Is Stored

EngRes Hub uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage. Account records, saved resources, download token records, resource activity, and deletion requests may be stored in Firebase. Uploaded PDF resources are stored in Firebase Storage.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the app, including Google Firebase for authentication, database, and storage, and Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising and rewarded ads.

We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the app, to prevent abuse, or to respond to valid legal requests.

5. Data Security

We use Firebase and Google services that support encrypted network communication. We also use app and database rules to limit access to user and admin data. No system is perfectly secure, but we aim to protect user information using reasonable technical and organizational measures.

6. Account Deletion

If you create an account, you can request account deletion from within the app by going to Profile > Request Account Deletion.

You can also use the external request page: Account Deletion Request.

After receiving a deletion request, we will review and process it. Deletion may include removing or anonymizing your account information, saved resources, download history, token records, and related app data, except where we are required or allowed to keep limited records for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

7. Children's Privacy

EngRes Hub is intended for teachers and education professionals, not as a child-directed app. If this changes, the privacy policy and app settings must be updated to comply with child privacy and advertising requirements.

8. Ads and Download Tokens

Users may watch rewarded ads to earn download tokens. Watching rewarded ads is optional, but download tokens may be required to download files to phone storage. Published resources can be read in the app without downloading.

Rewarded ad views are counted only for the purpose of determining whether a download token should be added to the user's account.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of the policy. If changes are significant, we may provide additional notice in the app or store listing.
