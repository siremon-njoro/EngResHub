EngRes Hub Account Deletion Request

English Resources for Teachers

Last updated: 2026-08-22

You can request deletion of your EngRes Hub account and associated app data from inside the app or by contacting support using the instructions below.

In-app method: Open EngRes Hub, sign in, go to Profile > Request Account Deletion, and submit the request.

External Request Method

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request to:

Email: siremondigitalsolutions@gmail.com

Use the subject line: EngRes Hub Account Deletion Request

Include the email address used for your EngRes Hub account. Do not send your password.

What Data May Be Deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account or sign-in access.
  • Your account profile, including name and email stored in the app database.
  • Your saved resources.
  • Your download token balance and token usage records.
  • Your download and reading activity records where appropriate.
  • Your pending deletion request after it is processed or marked complete.

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited records may be retained where required or allowed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, or operational reasons.

Processing Time

We aim to review deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible. You may be contacted if more information is needed to confirm the account.
